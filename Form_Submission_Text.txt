PROJECT TITLE
SafeShare AI: On-Device AI Privacy Guardian for Screen Sharing

BRIEF PROJECT DESCRIPTION (paste into the 500-character field)
SafeShare AI is a privacy-focused on-device AI solution that scans screenshots before screen sharing. It detects potentially sensitive text, faces and QR codes locally, calculates a privacy-risk score, and can blur, pixelate or blackout detected regions. The architecture is designed for Snapdragon-powered HP PCs with a Qualcomm AI Hub/ONNX deployment path, reducing cloud dependency and helping protect private information during meetings, presentations and recordings.
